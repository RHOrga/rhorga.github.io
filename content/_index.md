The <a href="https://www.hulks.de/">HULKs</a>, the robot soccer team from the Hamburg University of Technology, will host the 12th Robotic Hamburg Open Workshop (RoHOW) at TUHH from November 28 to November 30, 2025.

This event is an opportunity for RoboCup teams to connect and discuss the future of humanoid robotics and competitive robot soccer. RoHOW is set up to be a place for detailed discussions, workshops, and lectures. It allows experts to share their findings, talk about challenges, and exchange new ideas in a collaborative setting.

The workshop uses an "Open Space Conference" format, which means the program is flexible and guided by the participants' interests. While there will be talks on subjects like deep learning for computer vision and robot walking metrics, attendees will also have the chance to discuss the league's development and other current topics.

Industry partners and sponsors will also be there to show their latest projects in robotics.

Please save the date. A formal invitation will be sent to all teams soon.
